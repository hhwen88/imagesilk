# Hi, I'm building ImageSilk 👋

> **ImageSilk** — a privacy-first online toolkit for converting, compressing, and editing files. No signup wall, no ads, no data retention.

I'm a solo developer building **ImageSilk** ([imagesilk.com](https://imagesilk.com)) — a fast, private alternative to the file converter sites that are full of ads and keep your files.

---

## 🛠 What I'm building

| Category | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📄 **Document Convert** | PDF, Word, Excel, PPT, OFD, CAD conversions — high-quality output, batch friendly |
| ✏️ **Document Edit** | Remove / add watermarks, extract images, redact sensitive info in PDF & Word |
| 🖼 **Image Convert** | 16 output formats — JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, SVG, HEIC, PSD and more |
| 📦 **File Compress** | Compress PDF, Word, Excel, PPT and images while keeping the original format |
| 🎨 **Image Studio** | Crop & resize, one-click filters, and captions for e-commerce & social media |

All tools are **free to try** — guests get 2 conversions, and signing up unlocks 512KB uploads plus your history.

## 🔒 The privacy promise

- Files are transferred over **HTTPS** and **auto-deleted within 24 hours**
- No signup wall for trying the tools
- No training on your files, no selling your data
